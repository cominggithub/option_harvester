One row per Mon–Sun week, keyed on the expiry date — 2 months of expiries back through the farthest one. A week owns every position expiring in it: the legs still open (Unreal, on current marks) and the contracts of that same expiry already closed out — expired or bought back early (Realized, booked). Wins/Loss counts the winning and losing positions (Win % is wins ÷ positions with a P/L, so a position sitting exactly at zero counts in neither); Profit, Loss and Net P/L span both sides; FAIL marks a week whose loss outweighs its profit. Because a position is filed by its own expiry, closing a September short early does not move it to the week you paid — it stays on its September row, and every position is counted exactly once, so the rows sum to the totals. Click a row (marked ↓) to jump to that week in [By expiry · detail](http://127.0.0.1:19210/pnl-predict#expiries), where an expiry with nothing open left appears as a dashed, closed-only card. For P/L by trade date instead, see [Trans](http://127.0.0.1:19210/transactions).
